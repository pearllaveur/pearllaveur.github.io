From September to December 2025, I taught *MAT304: Multivariable functions and matrix calculus* to second-year undergraduate students in mechanical physics at the [DLST, Université Grenoble Alpes](https://formations.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/fr/catalogue-2021/licence-XA/licence-physique-IAI3PBC7/parcours-physique-mecanique-2e-annee-IAI4QJAF.html). This course covered topics such as study of critical points, diagonalisation, differential operators: gradient, Laplacian, rotational, divergence.
